# Finaxis — how to turn traffic into clients

The site is well-built and the SEO foundation is solid. Getting from "visitors" to "clients" now
depends on a handful of levers, ordered by impact ÷ effort. Items marked **[me]** I can do on the
site; **[you]** need your account/login or business input.

## Tier 1 — do these first (highest impact, lowest effort)
1. **[you] Google Search Console** — verify + submit sitemap so Google indexes you. Without it,
   Google traffic stays near zero. (3 clicks; I do the server side — see chat steps.)
2. **[you] Google Business Profile (Amsterdam)** — the fastest local-trust + "near me" win. Your
   schema is already primed (Amsterdam locality). Needs your postcard/phone verification.
3. **[me+you] Conversion tracking** — give me the Google Ads Conversion ID; I wire form/phone/
   WhatsApp events. Required before spending on ads. See `google-ads-campaign.md`.
4. **[you→me] Case studies** — the single biggest *conversion* lever for a new site. A CFO needs
   proof. Send me 2–3 anonymized stories (challenge → what you did → one metric) from your
   LinkedIn/CV and I'll build a proper case-study section + schema. Right now the site asserts
   credibility but doesn't *show* outcomes.

## Tier 2 — paid acquisition (fast, but costs money)
5. **[you+me] Google Ads** — launch NL Search first (€20/day), tightly targeted, pointed at the
   specific service pages (which now exist per market). Full plan in `google-ads-campaign.md`.
6. **[you] LinkedIn** — you're active there. Post the new kennisbank articles; LinkedIn outreach
   to target-profile CFOs is often the highest-converting channel for this exact niche.

## Tier 3 — on-site conversion optimisation (compounding)
7. **[me] Calendar booking** — if you use Calendly/Cal.com, give me the link and I'll add a
   "Book a 20-min call" button alongside the contact form. Removing the "wait for a reply" step
   measurably lifts B2B conversions.
8. **[me] Lower form friction** — keep required fields minimal (name + email + one line). Every
   extra required field drops completion.
9. **[me] Trust signals near every CTA** — "Operational in 5 business days · No long-term
   contract · Audit-ready" microcopy under buttons (partly present already).
10. **[me] Keep publishing kennisbank articles** — long-tail SEO compounds; cheapest durable
    lead source. I can draft ~1–2/week on winnable queries.

## Tier 4 — authority (slow, essential for competitive terms)
11. **[you] Backlinks** — LinkedIn company page, industry directories, partner/client sites,
    guest articles. This is what eventually ranks you for head terms like
    "debiteurenbeheer uitbesteden".

## What NOT to do
- No Display / Performance Max ads yet (wastes budget in a niche B2B context).
- Don't buy backlinks or use SEO "packages" — risks a Google penalty.
- Don't broaden ad keywords to broad match before you have conversion data.

## Realistic timeline
- **Week 1:** GSC + GBP live, conversion tracking, ads campaign 1 live → first paid leads possible.
- **Weeks 2–6:** ads optimised on real data; first organic long-tail rankings appear.
- **Months 2–4:** organic Google traffic becomes meaningful; case studies + reviews compound.
