About

Turning Complex Financial Processes Into Streamlined Operations

"We bring institutional-grade financial operations to businesses that need expert support — without the overhead of a full in-house team."

— Alexander Gevorgyan, founder, Finaxis

Finaxis is a specialist team with hands-on experience across automotive finance, fleet leasing, insurance, and fintech. With a track record spanning organisations including Fiat Chrysler Automobiles, Generali, Ayvens, and ALD Automotive, we have developed deep expertise in underwriting, accounts receivable, and CDD/KYC compliance.

Based in the Netherlands and available worldwide, Finaxis was built to solve a real problem: companies spending too much time and money on financial back-office functions that fall outside their core business. Whether you need a fully managed outsourcing partner or a specialist contractor, we deliver results with precision and full confidentiality.
